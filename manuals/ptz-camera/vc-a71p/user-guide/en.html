The VC-A71P is a 4K 30x PTZ camera designed for lecture capture, conference rooms and broadcast-grade production. This manual covers unboxing, basic operation and everyday settings.

Overview

VC-A71P outputs up to 4K/30p over HDMI, 3G-SDI and IP simultaneously, with 30x optical zoom and a 90° wide-angle mode. It supports PTZ presets, tally indicators, and remote control via IR, RS-232/RS-422 and network.

Package Contents

  • VC-A71P camera unit
  • IR remote control
  • Power adapter and regional power cord
  • Wall-mount bracket and screws
  • Quick start card

Setup & Connections

Connect video output (HDMI / 3G-SDI / LAN) to your switcher, capture device or network, then connect power last. The status LED blinks blue while the camera initializes and turns solid white once ready.

PortUse
HDMI OUTDirect display or capture card connection

Basic Operation

Use the IR remote or a joystick controller to pan, tilt and zoom. Press PRESET + a number key to save a camera position, and press the number key alone to recall it.

Up to 255 presets can be stored when controlling the camera over IP with VISCA-over-IP.

Troubleshooting

If the camera does not respond to the remote, confirm the camera ID on the remote matches the camera's DIP-switch setting. If there is no video signal, verify the output format selected on the camera matches what your downstream device expects.
