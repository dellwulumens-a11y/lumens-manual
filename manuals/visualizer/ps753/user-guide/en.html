The PS753 is a 4K desktop document camera for classrooms and meeting rooms, capturing documents, 3D objects and whiteboards with a single arm-mounted camera head.

Overview

PS753 offers a large capture area, adjustable-height camera arm, and built-in LED lighting for even document illumination. Output is available over HDMI and USB simultaneously.

Package Contents

  • PS753 document camera
  • USB cable and HDMI cable
  • Power adapter
  • Remote control

Setup & Connections

Unfold the camera arm fully before powering on, and connect HDMI to a projector/display or USB to a computer for use as a webcam-style capture device.

Basic Operation

Press AUTO FOCUS after repositioning a document. Use the zoom rocker on the base to frame the capture area, and the LED button to toggle the built-in lighting.

Troubleshooting

If the image is soft, confirm autofocus is enabled and the document is flat within the capture area. If the computer does not detect the camera over USB, try a different USB port or cable.
