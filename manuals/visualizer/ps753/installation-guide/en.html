This guide covers fixed desktop placement and AV system integration for the PS753.

Before You Start

  • Choose a stable, vibration-free surface for the base.
  • Allow enough overhead clearance for the arm to unfold fully.

Mounting

The PS753 is designed for desktop placement only. Do not clamp or mount the base to a moving surface, as this affects autofocus stability.

Wiring

CableRecommendation
PowerUse the supplied adapter only
VideoHDMI to display/projector, or USB to a PC

Installation Verification

After setup, confirm autofocus locks within 2 seconds when a document is placed, and that LED lighting is even across the capture area.
